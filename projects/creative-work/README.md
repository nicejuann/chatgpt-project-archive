# Creative projects (inventory only)

Several authored projects were recovered from ChatGPT Library, including the books **Beloved Son**, **Until Christ Is Formed**, **Where Are You**, and **All Our Days**, plus children's storybooks **Benny Bear Learns to Clean Up**, **Ollie Otter's Turn to Play**, **Tilly's Little Kindness Walk**, and **Milo and the Wobbly Tower**.

**The manuscript and ebook files are intentionally not published here.** A public code repository is not automatically an appropriate venue for unpublished, personal, or commercial manuscripts and illustrated artwork.
